Our Mission & History

Established in 2010, Bridges Academy Austin is a private school that has been serving the Austin community for over a decade. Our school is built on a foundation of academic excellence, social responsibility, and community engagement.

Our teaching approach is centered around project-based learning, which allows our students to explore complex concepts in a hands-on and collaborative manner. This approach not only fosters a love of learning but also helps our students develop essential skills in critical thinking, problem-solving, and communication.

At Bridges Academy Austin, we believe that a well-rounded education goes beyond academics. Our students have access to a range of extracurricular activities, including sports, music, art, and drama, which help them develop their passions and interests outside of the classroom.
