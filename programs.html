Academics & Enrichment

Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum designed to challenge, inspire, and prepare every student for success.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-Kindergarten program is designed for children aged 3-5 and provides a nurturing environment that fosters social, emotional, and cognitive growth. Our experienced teachers use play-based learning to introduce fundamental concepts in literacy, numeracy, and science.

📚

Elementary

Our Elementary program serves students from Kindergarten to 5th Grade and provides a rigorous and engaging education that meets the needs of every learner. Our experienced teachers use a variety of instructional strategies to support students in developing a deep understanding of core subjects like math, science, and language arts.

🎓

Middle/Upper

Our Middle and Upper School program serves students from 6th to 8th Grade and provides a challenging and supportive education that prepares students for high school and beyond. Our experienced teachers use project-based learning and other innovative strategies to help students develop critical thinking, problem-solving, and communication skills.

💻

STEM

Our STEM program is designed to provide students with a comprehensive education in science, technology, engineering, and math. Our experienced teachers use hands-on and project-based learning to introduce students to fundamental concepts in coding, robotics, and engineering.

🎨

Arts

Our Arts program provides students with a range of opportunities to explore their creativity and develop their artistic talents. Our experienced teachers use a variety of instructional strategies to support students in developing skills in music, art, drama, and dance.

🏀

Athletics

Our Athletics program provides students with a range of opportunities to develop their physical skills and compete with other schools in a fun and supportive environment. Our experienced coaches use a variety of instructional strategies to support students in developing skills in sports like soccer, basketball, and volleyball.
